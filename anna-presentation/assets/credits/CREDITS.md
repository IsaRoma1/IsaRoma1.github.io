## Future assets

Before adding any external model or texture:

1. Confirm that commercial use is allowed.
2. Reject CC BY-NC and assets without a clear license.
3. Add the author, source URL, license, and local filename to this file.
4. Add files over 20 MB to the project asset manifest.
